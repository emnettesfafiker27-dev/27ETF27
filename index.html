About our school

ስለ ትምህርት ቤታችን

Our mission

To teach children and youth the faith, scripture and traditions of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahdo Church, and to help them grow in Christian character.

ሕፃናትንና ወጣቶችን የኦርቶዶክስ ተዋሕዶ ቤተክርስቲያንን ሃይማኖት፣ ትውፊትና ሥነ ምግባር ማስተማር።

Faith · እምነት

Rooted in the teaching of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahdo Church.

Hope · ተስፋ

Growing in the hope that Christ gives to every student.

Love · ፍቅር

Serving one another and our church with love.
